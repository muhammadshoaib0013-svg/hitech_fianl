# Owner browser acceptance checklist

- [ ] Inspect homepage on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Check all page links, mobile navigation, footer and cart drawer.
- [ ] Open product gallery thumbnails and switch images.
- [ ] Switch the four solution-finder tabs; verify the image and messaging change.
- [ ] Add a printer to the cart, increase/decrease quantity, close/reopen cart, and confirm quantity persists across page navigation.
- [ ] Remove the item and verify empty cart.
- [ ] Confirm checkout clearly says **not connected**, and no fake order can be created.
- [ ] Verify inquiry is copied, not claimed to be sent.
- [ ] Replace concept visuals with accurate images of the actual selling model.
- [ ] Confirm actual SKU, paper width/type, connectivity/app, included parts, colors, battery, warranty and support.
- [ ] Approve exact selling price, VAT/taxes, shipping fees, available cities and checkout provider.
- [ ] Provide official phone, WhatsApp, email and social profiles.
- [ ] Approve live shipping, returns, privacy and sales terms.
- [ ] Connect ecommerce provider and independently verify real sandbox test orders before production launch.
- [ ] Verify analytics consent/tracking, basic accessibility, security and deployment configuration.
