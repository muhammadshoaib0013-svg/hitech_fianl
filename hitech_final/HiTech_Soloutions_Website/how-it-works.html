THE SIMPLE SCIENCE

No ink cartridges. Just thermal paper.

Thermal printers use heat-sensitive paper rather than conventional ink cartridges. That makes them useful for quick monochrome prints.

The correct roll size and supported adhesive paper type depend on the exact printer being sold.
